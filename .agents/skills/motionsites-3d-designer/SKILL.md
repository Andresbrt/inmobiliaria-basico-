## MotionSites Aesthetic Philosophy
1. **Full-Bleed Panoramic Immersion**:
   - The viewport is not an isolated card; it dominates 100vw and 100vh.
   - Micro-parallax responds to cursor movement and scroll position with subtle ease.
2. **Editorial Hierarchy**:
   - Headlines: Elegant high-contrast serif (Cormorant Garamond, Italiana, Playfair) with italic accents.
   - Labels & Specs: Monospaced or uppercase sans-serif (Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit) with tracking (0.15em - 0.25em).
   - Floating components: Ultra-thin 1px border lines, frosted glass (backdrop-filter: blur(20px)), and luminous status indicators.
3. **Atmospheric Transitions**:
   - Seamless transition between times of day (Radiant Caribbean Noon, Amber Golden Sunset, Starry Bioluminescent Night).
   - Organic elements: Ocean wave displacement, light refraction, caustics, and audio ambience.
4. **Performance & Optimization**:
   - 60 FPS WebGL rendering with requestAnimationFrame, tone mapping, and responsive resize observers.
   - Zero layout shifts and instant loading.
